Design A · The Journey

How math becomes music

Scroll. One picture on the left changes as you read — from a typed equation to a shape, a moving scanner, a pitch, and finally a chord. Almost no text blocks; the picture does the explaining.

Feels like: a short film you scroll through. Best for: first-time visitors and sharing. Trade-off: longest page, least hands-on; needs good mobile care (the picture stays pinned at the top while you read).
Design B · The Instrument

Turn the dials. Hear the maths.

One screen, no scrolling story. Move a dial, the curve changes, and the card that explains it lights up. Press play to hear exactly that curve.

Design C · The Signal Chain

Five steps from equation to sound

A map of what MathSonify does, with the data flowing left to right. Tap any step to see it working. It plays through the steps on its own until you take over.

Feels like: a technical diagram that comes alive. Best for: curious, technical visitors; fits one screen. Trade-off: more "engineering" than "emotion"; the demos are small.
